Welcome to Sibiya's Chicken Farming

At Sibiya Chicken Farming, we believe in delivering more than just chicken — we bring nourishment, trust, and care to every table. Our promise is to raise healthy chickens with kindness, using eco-friendly methods that protect our planet.

Our mission at Sibiya's chicken farming is to provide affordable, fresh, and quality chicken while building strong relationships with our customers. We’re passionate about growing our community by offering good jobs and helping families enjoy reliable, wholesome food.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted chicken supplier in KZN by growing sustainably and putting our customers first.
