Project Overview

Sibiya’s Chicken Farming started as a backyard initiative in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, with a mission to deliver fresh, high-quality poultry to the local community. Today, the agribusiness supplies local households, spaza shops, butcheries, caterers, and eateries with fresh Ross 308 broilers, point-of-lay hens, fresh eggs, and organic poultry fertilizer.

Mission Statement

To provide affordable, fresh, and quality chicken while raising healthy birds in ethical, clean conditions and using eco-friendly methods that protect our planet.

Vision Statement

To become the most trusted poultry supplier in KZN through sustainable growth, compassionate livestock care, biosecurity excellence, and direct community nourishment.

Target Audience

Primary (B2C): Local households, families, and health-conscious buyers seeking fresh, clean protein.

Commercial (B2B): Local food retailers, spaza shops, butcheries, caterers, and restaurants needing reliable bulk supply.

Institutional: Schools and community feeding schemes sourcing affordable bulk protein.

Website Goals and Objectives

Build Brand Awareness & Trust: Highlight ethical animal care, SAPA biosecurity standards, farm hygiene, and customer reviews.

Drive Direct Sales & Lead Generation: Integrate instant "Order via WhatsApp" functionality and dynamic bulk quote inquiry calculators.

Display Product Offerings Clearly: Showcase live broilers, processed birds, specific portions, layer eggs, and composted fertilizer transparently.

Streamline Customer Service: Provide interactive query forms, direct business lines (064 735 9333), and automated AI assistance.

Key Features and Functionality

Multi-Tab SPA Architecture: Simulates multi-page navigation across index.html (Home), about.html (About Us), services.html (Services & Products), contact.html (Contact Us), and technical.html (Robots & Sitemap).

Interactive Batch Profitability & Order Calculator: Real-time computation of gross revenue, operational expenses, and net profit margins based on custom bird counts and prices.

Direct WhatsApp Query Integration: Auto-compiles order details into pre-filled WhatsApp messages to 064 735 9333.

SAPA Biosecurity Audit Checklist: Interactive compliance tracker checking daily farm protocols.

SEO & Technical File Inspector: Interactive modal and downloadable previews of robots.txt and sitemap.xml.

Dark/Light Mode Theme Toggle: Complete visual adaptation with persistent theme switching.

Timeline and Milestones

Business Setup Timeline

Month 1: Land Preparation & Housing Setup (Structures, insulation, thermal control).

Month 2: Equipment Installation & Supplier Integration (Automatic feeders, drinkers, DOC contracts).

Month 3: Trial Batch (100–200 chicks) & Market Testing (Buyer feedback & FCR tracking).

Month 4+: Full-Scale Staggered Production (500+ birds per batch cycle).

Key Performance Benchmarks

Mortality Rate Target: Kept strictly below 5% per batch cycle.

Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR): Maintained between 1.5 and 1.8 kg of feed per 1 kg of body mass gain.

Turnaround Time: 50-to-55 days per house cycle (including cleaning downtime).
